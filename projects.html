Economic Growth &
Environmental Health

Analysed global panel data across approximately 190 countries to study the relationship between growth, PM2.5 pollution, and life expectancy.

  • Applied non-linear fixed-effects econometric models.
  • Identified Environmental Kuznets Curve turning points.
  • Developed forecasts for pollution decline during economic transitions.
Read the case study ↓Repository ↗

The question

Where does the relationship turn?

I analysed global panel data to study the relationship between economic growth, PM2.5 pollution, and life expectancy, looking for the point at which the environmental cost of growth begins to change direction.

The method

Model the shape, not just the average.

I applied non-linear fixed-effects econometric models with country and year effects and quadratic income terms. This made it possible to identify Environmental Kuznets Curve turning points and develop forecasts for pollution decline through economic transitions.

The result

A measurable turning point across a broad panel.

The analysis identified turning points in the income-pollution relationship and produced a way to discuss the direction of pollution after the peak rather than treating the trend as linear.

Video Game
Market Analysis

Processed and analysed more than 7,500 video game records using data cleaning, preprocessing, and exploratory analysis.

  • Compared Random Forest, Logistic Regression, and Decision Trees.
  • Achieved 79% accuracy and 0.87 ROC AUC.
  • Identified platform availability and user scores as key predictors.
Read the case study ↓Repository ↗

The question

What actually predicts performance?

I processed and explored more than 7,500 video game records, using data cleaning, preprocessing, and exploratory analysis to understand which variables carried useful signal.

The models

Compare different ways to classify.

I built and evaluated Random Forest, Logistic Regression, and Decision Tree classifiers on the same analysis workflow, keeping the comparison focused on the resulting performance rather than the model name.

The result

79% accuracy and 0.87 ROC AUC.

The analysis identified platform availability and user scores as key predictors across the records, giving the project a concrete result to evaluate and explain.
