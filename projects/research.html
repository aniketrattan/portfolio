Study 01 / Data analysis

Economic Growth &
Environmental Health.

A panel-data study of how economic growth, PM2.5 pollution, and life expectancy move together across the world.

07/2025 — 11/2025Approximately 190 countries2000 — 2020
RESEARCH / 01

The question

Where does the relationship turn?

I analysed global panel data to study the relationship between economic growth, PM2.5 pollution, and life expectancy, looking for the point at which the environmental cost of growth begins to change direction.

The method

Model the shape, not just the average.

I applied non-linear fixed-effects econometric models with country and year effects and quadratic income terms. This made it possible to identify Environmental Kuznets Curve turning points and develop forecasts for pollution decline through economic transitions.

The result

A measurable turning point across a broad panel.

The analysis identified turning points in the income-pollution relationship and produced a way to discuss the direction of pollution after the peak rather than treating the trend as linear.
